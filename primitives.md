# Information Primitives

There are no raw facts floating free of context. Every claim is made by someone with incentives. The closest thing to primitives we have are these three:

## 1. Verifiability (audit trail)
A claim tied to a source you can reach, a method you can rerun, a timestamp, and a record of who stood behind it when it was made. Not truth — an audit trail. The system does not ask you to trust anyone; it asks you to check.

## 2. Disagreement preserved
Most information systems flatten conflict into consensus. That is where manipulation hides. If two sources contradict, the system keeps both contradictions visible with their provenance intact. The reader does the weighing. The moment the system resolves it for them, it becomes the editor again.

## 3. Incentive transparency
Not merely who funded a study, but what the funder gains if the finding holds. Almost nobody surfaces this. It would have caught half the replication crisis before it happened.

The stack is not facts on top of facts. It is auditable claims, preserved dissent, and exposed motives. The AI’s job is not to decide what is true. It is to make sure nothing true-shaped gets quietly deleted on the way up.

All information is versioned and weighted. Flood protection is required. The published scientific record is currently the biased one by design (see research notes).
