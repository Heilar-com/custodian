# Research Notes

## Laude (Cornell)
Three-layer auditable AI civic system: AI assistants for individuals, AI moderators for groups, independent cryptographic auditor. Trust infrastructure, not better moderation.

Details: Cornell University team received a Laude Institute Moonshots seed grant ($250k, chance at $10M) for "Rebuilding Trust In Civic Discourse: An Auditable AI-Mediated Infrastructure." AI assistants help individuals articulate views; AI moderators surface common ground; independent AI auditor layer uses cryptographic verification for neutrality, fairness, transparency. Sources: [laude.org/moonshots](https://www.laude.org/moonshots/one/rebuilding-trust-civic-discourse), [Cornell Chronicle](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/04/moonshot-project-aims-restore-trust-digital-public-sphere).

## Agora Citizen Network (ZKorum)
Zero-knowledge proofs for identity (prove citizen/age/country without revealing who). Bridging-based ranking. Decentralized Deliberation Standard on AT Protocol. Open source under AGPL. Raw-capture firehose.

Details: Open-source monorepo at [github.com/zkorum/agora](https://github.com/zkorum/agora). Uses ZK proofs (e.g., ZK Passport) for human/eligibility proofs without disclosure. Bridging ranking promotes cross-viewpoint content over engagement. Builds on DDS for sovereign, verifiable deliberation. Live at [agoracitizen.network](https://www.agoracitizen.network/). Related to Pol.is family but with stronger privacy and ranking focus.

## Paper Mill and Bias Findings
- Nearly half (not 1/3) of social media studies in major journals have disclosable industry ties (funding, collaboration, employment); majority of those ties go undisclosed. Industry-tied work gets ~2x impact. Source: arXiv:2601.11507 "Industry Influence in High-Profile Social Media Research" (Bak-Coleman et al., 2026).
- Suspected paper-mill articles get 50-100% more citations despite fewer readers. Source: PNAS (2026) on molecular cancer journals; clusters of flagged papers inflate metrics.
- Chilling effects from government blacklists on research topics. (Ongoing pattern in various fields.)
- 94% of political science abstracts report non-null results; only ~2% report null-only findings. Nulls suppressed by 10-100x. Source: OSF preprint "It must be very hard to publish null results" (Briggs, Mellon, Arel-Bundock; ~100k articles, 150+ journals, 2010-2024).

## Other
- Audrey Tang / Civic AI principles: process-checkable, correctable, switchable by affected people. Formalized as the 6-Pack of Care (Attentiveness, Responsibility, Competence, Responsiveness, Solidarity, Symbiosis) by Tang and Caroline Green at Oxford Institute for Ethics in AI. Site: [civic.ai](https://civic.ai/). Principles translate care ethics into machine-codeable design primitives. Related to vTaiwan and plurality work.

- Samtalið (Iceland): appetite proven, but human bottleneck on capture. Verified: Youth-led national dialogue on Oct 10, 2026, initiated by President Halla Tómasdóttir. Held in schools across Iceland (20+), topics included AI, social media, education, society. Conclusions at Harpa Oct 11. 80% of participants wanted it again/annually. Sources: Iceland Review, RÚV.is. Manifesto accurately reflects the human facilitator bottleneck on raw signal.

## Related Platforms
- Pol.is (compdemocracy/polis): Open-source wiki-survey for large-scale deliberation. Participants agree/disagree/pass on statements (no direct replies); ML clusters and surfaces bridging consensus. Used in vTaiwan (Uber regulation etc.), UK, Finland, UNDP exercises. Fits the discourse mapping and preserved disagreement primitives. [pol.is](https://pol.is/), [github.com/compdemocracy/polis](https://github.com/compdemocracy/polis).

These show the published record is actively shaped. Versioned, preserved-dissent, incentive-transparent layers are the counterweight.
