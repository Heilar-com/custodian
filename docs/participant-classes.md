# Discourse first, participant classes

> Status: design claim (Markús, Grok.com briefing, 2026-10-23).

## Discourse first
Topics come from what people say. Research is the mirror: claims from discussion are matched against research works (for example via OpenAlex funding fields, topic vectors and nearest-neighbour matching between claims and works). Tooling is a proposal, not tested.

## Participant classes
Politicians, students, retirees and others are **classes of participant**, not separate layers. They differ in incentives and in what it costs them to say something. Politicians' citations go through the same research-integrity checks as anyone else's.

## Seed path (order from the briefing)
1. Parliamentary (Alþingi) transcripts: speaker-tagged claims, fallacy and funding checks, a card per claim, and a diff to show how claims shift over time.
2. Podcasts (needs speech recognition).
3. Anonymous citizen tables.

Note: the README lists podcasts after citizen tables. Order to be confirmed.

## Voice limits
Voice processing is used only to prove consent and check capture quality. It is never used to label intent, personality or character.
