# Custodian Organism Slide Deck Outline

> Note: The organism metaphor (research integrity gaps as cascade, nodes as independent verification) is for deeper exploration and visual storytelling. Entry-level materials use clinical language: coherence, integrity gaps, incentive transparency, preserved disagreement.

## Slide 1: Title
**The Custodian Organism: An Auditable Human Knowledge Network**
Transparent discourse · Preserved dissent · Incentive transparency · Against incoherent or integrity-compromised information

## Slide 2: Inception
From Samtalið (Iceland Oct 10 2026, President Halla Tómasdóttir) and the manifesto:
- Appetite for conversation proven; raw signal lost to human facilitators.

- Aggregation without editor; preserve dark regions.

## Slide 3: Core Primitives (DNA)
1. **Verifiability**: Audit trail – source, method, timestamp, who stood behind it. Check, don't trust.
2. **Disagreement Preserved**: Keep contradictions visible with provenance. Reader weighs; system does not resolve.
3. **Incentive Transparency**: What the funder/actor gains if the finding holds.
AI’s job: prevent quiet deletion of true-shaped material, not decide truth.

## Slide 4: The Human Knowledge Network as Organism
Layers of flow: Research → Media → Politics → Public Discourse → Education → Echo Chambers.
Whole system as living organism. Integrity gaps in research as cascading coherence issues. Independent nodes as verification and audit layer.

## Slide 5: Research Layer – Integrity Gaps
- ~49% of high-profile social media research has industry ties (majority undisclosed). arXiv:2601.11507.
- Suspected paper-mill articles: 50-100% more citations despite fewer readers (attributed to PNAS 2026; not verified, see research notes).
- Political science abstracts: 94% non-null; ~2% null-only (nulls suppressed 10-100x).
Trace affiliations: funders → authors → citations → media → politicians. Incentive transparency exposes motives.

## Slide 6: High-reach participant class
Politicians are a high-reach participant class with an asymmetric cost of words. Congressional / parliamentary transcripts run through the same integrity database as every other claim.
- Logical fallacy detection (DISPUTool 3.0 / RAG approaches on ElecDeb60to20) produces challengeable per-claim flags.
- Speaker truthfulness scores are computed on demand from those flags, erasable with an end date, and never stored permanently.
- Use of verified and integrity-compromised sources is read on demand from the same challengeable per-claim flags.
Accountability: flag claims rooted in research with integrity gaps or high fallacy density.

## Slide 7: Cascade of Coherence Issues
Integrity gaps in research → media amplification → political speech → public discourse → schools/education → echo chambers (bubbles with interpretive membranes).
Like flawed AI agent factory cascading into more flawed agents.
Collective discourse with accumulating unresolved contradictions; dark regions show where conversation doesn't go.

## Slide 8: Public Discourse Engine
Connects raw capture to the knowledge network.
Flags statements based on research with integrity gaps, using challengeable per-claim fallacy flags. A speaker-level reading is computed on demand from those flags, erasable with an end date, and never stored permanently.
Aggregates discourse maps preserving disagreement and dark regions.
Tamper-evident audit of what was recorded and erased (not a proof of neutrality).
Informs public: “this claim rests on research with undisclosed industry ties.”

## Slide 9: Network of Independent Custodian Nodes
Cooperate but maintain separation (error correction via network).
Self-care floors. Glass-box transparency + cryptographic proofs.
Forest from seed: Iceland start, grow like independent trees.
VitNet-style contract spine for civic ops.
