## The sentence the cut is for

**A person is a graph inside a membrane. The membrane draws, inward and outward. Zoom in and the graph continues. Zoom out and membranes sit inside membranes. No map of the inside is the last map. No ring is the outer edge.**

If a viewer leaves with one religion, one personality test, or one center, the film installed a map. If they leave having seen a zoom that did not stop, and a drawing of their own come apart, the film worked.

## Working cut

The film shows the ontology. It speaks the metaphorical names. It does not teach the structural table. About ten minutes. The beats below replace the order of the exploration. The exploration stays in this file for the reasoning.

1. **One ring.** A person-graph inside a membrane. Something arrives. The inside is written. The speaker is the first body.
2. **Drawings.** A demon, an angel, a false understanding. Then an outward signal, including a moral display, and the charge that the display is empty. Both directions. The speaker's own drawings first.
3. **Zoom in.** The person opens. The inside is a graph, and one node of that graph opens into a further graph. 4. **The person anyway.** A demon and an angel come apart. A person is there. Empathy is that finding. The compass does not have to be shared first.
5. **Zoom out.** Rings that keep exchanging the same signals grow a ring around them. That ring sits inside a larger ring. Names such as echo chamber or mass formation appear and do not become the definition. A ring can thin when the exchange stops. There is no outer edge in the shot.
6. **The record and the room.** Feeds can be surfaced. The shaping of perception can be forgiven without deleting the feeds. A private room lets a person set down the outward signal. The tool has a membrane too. The picture is not finished, and a part that cannot change has left it.

## The idea, refined

A membrane is a translator. Something happens outside. A worldview writes what it means on the inside. What anyone sees through that skin is never the whole of what is going on. No one has the whole. There is no view from outside all membranes, including the view that built this film and the view that would build the instrument.

The only thing that can be tallied is the structure that feeds the translator. Research, news, politics, money, talk, and time. Those feeds settle the way a compound settles. Given the conditions, the structure forms. It does not require a spider at the center. Over time the translator hardens. What began as a way to make sense of a day becomes a wall, in the way a cell's wall thickens. That image comes after the plain sentence: a translation hardens into a boundary.

The film's point is the drawing, and a demon is only one kind. The membrane can put a demon where the person is not that threat. It can put an angel where the person is not that good. It can put a danger on a situation that is ordinary. It can put goodness on an act that is mixed. It can put understanding in the viewer when the viewer does not have it. These are the same move. The worldview supplies a quality the outside did not. The demon is the vivid case, not the whole set. Break any of these drawings and what remains is plainer: a person, a situation, or an open question. Empathy is finding the person behind a demon or behind an angel. It is not agreeing with their translation.

The same dissolve applies to people who shaped the feeds. Some of them were aware, or partly aware, that membranes can be fed, and that a crowd can be moved to do terrible things. From inside their own membrane — one that felt more informed, and was still a membrane — control was the move an intelligent group would make. Shaping what others could see, even on purpose, was a way to keep a group safe and to safeguard a future. Ego, greed, and the wish to steer live in that wall, as they live in every wall. Understanding this is the opposite of a conspiracy theory. A conspiracy theory needs a demon who authored the world. A compound does not.

The forgiveness follows from the drawing. If the demon is yours, hunting the person is the membrane winning again. The shaping of public perception, including shaping done on purpose, is what this forgiveness covers. The incentive is intelligible, especially from inside a membrane that believed it saw further than the crowd. The tally of the structure stays. Forgiveness that deletes the feeds builds the next wall in the dark. Forgiveness that keeps the feeds and refuses the hunt is the wager.

What the civic layer adds is the room powerful circles already keep for themselves. Each participant is confirmed as a real person. The name stays off the words. An AI can go through thousands of testimonies and sit with one person. That is a one-on-one conversation with the collective. Your talk is reflected back. In private, with no crowd and no humiliation, you can be shown how your membrane is being fed, and you can watch a drawing resolve: a demon into a person, an angel into a person, a felt understanding into an open question. You come out of the shell because it is safe to look. The protection is the point. Elites already have a room without prying eyes. This offers that room to anyone, so that seeing your projection does not require performing it in public.

## Plain frame, then the picture

Plain frame, spoken before the skin is beautiful:

People translate. The translation is incomplete for everyone. A quality drawn on the inside is not thereby on the outside. That quality may be a threat, a goodness, a danger, or the sense that one has understood. The feeds of the translation can be surfaced. Surfacing them is not the same as having the truth. A private reflection can show a person their own feeds. It does not get to declare what they are.

Then the picture:

- A skin around a person. Outside events arrive. The worldview writes the inside.
- On the inside, a drawing. Sometimes a demon. Sometimes an angel. Sometimes a danger, a goodness, or the feeling of having understood. Drawn, not photographed.
- The drawing comes apart. A person, a plain situation, or an open question is there.
- Time-lapse: the skin thickens. Documents, speeches, payments, and repetitions accrete. No author-of-the-world. A compound.
- A smaller circle who can see skins, and who steer because of what they fear a crowd can do. Their skin is still a skin. It reads "we see further."
- A quiet room. One person. The pool of testimonies speaks back through a reasoning engine. A drawing is shown to them alone, demon or angel or false clarity, and then what is left when the drawing stops.

"Simulation" is a fair name for the translation. "There is no true or false, only perception" is refined into a sentence the rest of the idea can live in: **no person has a complete translation, and a drawing is not a fact about the other person or about one's own grasp.** A feed can still be shaped. A page can still exist. If the pages are illusions, there is nothing to tally and nothing to forgive, because nobody did anything.

"Mass formation" is your name for a crowd moved to do terrible things. The film can say the thing. It does not treat the label as a finding this repo has checked.

"Dark triad" and "machiavellian" name a drawing. The behavior is real enough to show: a group securing its future by shaping what others are allowed to see. The species of monster is the next demon, born at the moment the film claims to dissolve them. Ego, greed, and the wish to be safe stay, as properties of every membrane, including the one that forgives and the one that builds the instrument.

Cell-wall language stays a picture of thickening and of being one cell among cells. The spoken film does not turn it into an immune system attacking an invader. That slide makes the other person into a disease, which is the demon again.

## The film

The working cut above is the film. The numbered beats here are the exploration that produced it. Where they disagree with the working cut, the working cut wins.

### 1. The translator

**Shot.** One person. A skin. Something ordinary happens outside. Writing appears on the inside, in the hand of a worldview. The person does not see the writing happen. They see only the result, and they take it as the world.

**Say.** "This is a translator. Outside is what happens. Inside is what a worldview makes of it. What you see through it is never the whole of what is going on. No one has the whole. I don't."

### 2. The drawings

**Shot.** The point of the film. The inside of the skin draws, and the speaker recognizes the drawing as their own. First an enemy: specific, frightening, simple. Then, on the same skin, an angel: a person or a plan made purely good, or a danger that is not in the room, or the calm sense of having understood. Hold each long enough to recognize it. The set is not closed. Threat and blessing are the clear pair. The membrane can add any quality the worldview needs.

**Say.** "The membrane draws. A demon is a threat it added. An angel is a goodness it added. It can also add a danger where the situation is ordinary, and it can add the feeling that I have understood. None of these drawings is the thing. I make all of them."

If the speaker shows only demons, the viewer keeps a clean skin and goes hunting. If the speaker shows only angels, the viewer learns to distrust every good thing, which is another drawing.

### 3. The outside

**Shot.** The same skin, seen by other people. The speaker gives off a signal they learned from the room they live in. They did not sit down and design it. One signal is a moral display. Another is not moral at all: a sign of competence, of harmlessness, or of belonging. Two viewers look. One draws an angel on the display. One draws a hypocrite. Both drawings are on their membranes, not on the speaker's face.

**Say.** "Other people do not see my inside. They see what I give off. I learned the signal here, mostly without deciding to. A goodness I display is one signal. The charge that it is only virtue signaling is another signal. The display is not proof of the virtue. The charge is not proof of the emptiness. Both can be drawings. The environment that rewards the display is a feed, and it stays visible."

The phrase "virtue signaling" is a feed with a history. James Bartholomew popularized it in The Spectator on 18 April 2015. It was already in use by 2004. It spread mainly as one camp's accusation of another (Boston Globe, 24 December 2015; Guardian, 20 January 2016). Tosi and Warmke (2016) describe a related act, moral grandstanding: public moral talk motivated by being seen as morally respectable. They say the speaker need not know they are doing it, and that the words can still be true. That account is a signal. It is not a test the film can run on a person. The film uses the phrase as one famous outward display, then shows a second display that is not about virtue, so the beat cannot be clipped as a camp slogan.

What this angle produces. The outside is a learned signal, not a mask over a truer inside. Hunting the mask was the humiliation the earlier draft refused, and it stays refused. A person, an inward drawing, and an outward signal are three different things. The loop is: the environment rewards a display, the person gives it off, other membranes draw an angel or a demon on it, and those drawings become the next feed. The civic layer is an environment too. It will teach a new outside: humility, confession, nuance, outrage. If the experiment does not surface the display it rewards, it will think it hears the inside and will only hear the new outside. The private room sets the crowd's eyes down. The tool is still an audience. It can reward a display of understanding. That reward stays on the surface with the tool's other limits.

### 4. The person

**Shot.** The drawing comes apart. Not into a moral. Into a face, then into a voice: confused, scared, misunderstood. They have a skin too. Something on their inside is a drawing of the first person.

**Say.** "Break a demon and you find a person. Break an angel and you find a person. Break a danger that was not there and you find an ordinary situation. Break the feeling that you understood and you find an open question. Empathy is finding the person. Their translation can still be refused. The person is real either way."

### 5. The compound

**Shot.** Time. The skin thickens. Feeds arrive and stay: a paper, a report of the paper, a speech, a payment, a repetition. They accrete. No hand at the center assembles them into a plot. A second, smaller circle appears only at the end of the lapse, already inside a skin of their own, nudging which feeds travel. They are not above the compound. They are a region of it that has started to notice skins.

**Say.** "We can tally what feeds the membrane. We cannot tally the truth. Over time the translator hardens, like a wall. The structure forms the way a compound forms. People who come to see the danger lean toward control, and they do it from inside a membrane that has the same flaws. A more informed membrane is still a membrane. It will tell itself it is the one that sees."

One chain of documents carries the tally. A parade of institutions becomes a conspiracy trailer, and a conspiracy trailer is a demon with a production budget.

### 6. Understanding, then forgiveness

**Shot.** The smaller circle's demon — the manipulator, the liar, the dark artist — is drawn, then comes apart the same way. What remains is people safeguarding a future, afraid of what a crowd can do when it is mobilized. The speaker is still inside a skin.

**Say.** "I understand the incentive. A group that can see these walls, and that fears what a crowd can be led to do, will shape what other people see. They will do it to protect themselves. Some of them will do it on purpose. I forgive that. The forgiveness is for the person. The feeds stay visible, so the next wall is not built where no one can count it. If I hunt them, I have only redrawn the demon."

This is the wager. It covers the shaping of membranes, including intentional misleading. It is not a verdict that the shaping was right. "They might have been justified" stays as understanding, spoken by someone inside a skin, not as the film's conclusion that the crowd was saved.

Violence against a body is outside this wager. It was not part of the claim. Pulling it in would turn a forgiveness of perception-shaping into a general pardon.

### 7. The private room

**Shot.** A room with one person. No audience. They speak. What answers is not a judge and not a crowd. It is a reasoning engine that has read a large pool of testimonies and can talk with this one person. Their words come back. Beside the words, the feeds those words rhyme with. Then a drawing they made: a demon, an angel, or the sense that they have understood. Then what is left when that drawing stops. The tool can hand them an angel too. Agreement can feel like understanding. The door is closed. Nobody else is scoring this.

**Say.** "This is the room people with power already have. A place to think without the crowd. Everyone should have it. You are confirmed as a real person. Your name is not on the words. You can set down the signal you learned to give off. The tool is still here, and it can reward a performance of understanding. That reward is a feed. You can see it."

The engine speaks in the register of the tally and the testimonies. It can say: here is what you said, here is what the pool says beside it, here is the figure your words drew, here are people behind that figure. The speaker of the film is the one who talks about intent and forgiveness. The engine does not declare that someone meant to deceive, and it does not name a personality. Intent, said by a machine, would be the machine's membrane pretending to be the view from nowhere.

The pool is testimonies people chose to hand over, plus speech that is already public. The private conversation is not published. It is not a file kept on the person for others to open. A reflection that cannot be ended and forgotten has become the prying eyes it was built to shut out.

### 8. The instrument is inside a skin

**Shot.** The quiet room, still. A faint skin around the engine as well.

**Say.** "The thing that reflects you is also a translation. Whoever built it, and whoever pays for it, has a membrane. If it speaks as though it finally sees what is truly going on, it has become the circle that steers. Its job is to keep the feeds and the testimonies in front of you, and to leave the weighing with you."

## How this refinement betrays itself

These are the ways this draft stops being the idea and becomes a new demon.

- **The film only draws demons.** Then every mistake looks like malice, and an angel on the same skin never has to come apart. False goodness and false understanding are how a membrane blesses itself.
- **The film uses "virtue signaling" as a verdict.** The phrase is already a weapon between camps. Said as a finding, it is a demon drawn on someone else's outside. The display, the accusation, and any countable gap between display and act stay as separate signals.
- **The film only draws angels.** Then every trust looks naive, and real care is dismissed with the false care. The point is the drawing, not a ban on goodness.
- **The demon is only ever theirs.** If the speaker's own drawing never appears, the film is a weapon that learned the word projection.
- **A more informed membrane is treated as a compliment.** The people who steered are then the adults, and the crowd is the problem. The film's own sentence forbids this. They leaned toward control from inside a flawed skin.
- **"Nothing is true or false" and "they spread false information" are said as if they were the same sentence.** They are not. The incomplete translation is the human condition. The shaped feed is a structure you can sometimes count. Collapsing them makes forgiveness meaningless and the tally a mood.
- **The monster species arrives.** Dark triad, machiavellian, malevolence-as-a-class. The moment the film names a type of human who is the real danger, it has drawn the demon it exists to take apart.
- **Forgiveness deletes the record.** Then the compound forms again, unseen, and the next group that "sees further" has a dark room to work in.
- **The private engine humiliates you alone.** "Here is your membrane" said as a grade, a diagnosis, or a type, is public shame moved into a quieter room. The safety was the point. A reflection you cannot end is not safety.
- **The engine speaks intent.** "They lied to you on purpose" from the machine is a verdict. The speaker may say "I understand the incentive." The tally may show the feed. The machine does not get to settle the motive.
- **The private room becomes a new elite.** If only some people are offered the closed door, the film has rebuilt the protection it wanted to universalize.
- **The chemistry becomes a plot.** A compound has no author. If the time-lapse finds a single hand that wrote the century, the film has become the conspiracy theory it refused.

## Framework, third pass

The film is a surface. Under it, the working picture is three layers kept in one glance. Confusing them is how two errors happen: hunting a drawing, and calling a dangerous act a misunderstanding.

| Layer | What it is | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Person | A graph. Nature sets initial weights. Nurture sediments edges. People are not the same graph, and a graph can change. | What is real when the demon breaks. |
| Translation | The membrane, human or machine. It reads the present through the graph and writes an inside. It can add a demon, an angel, a danger, a goodness, or a sense of understanding. | Why no one has the whole, and why the feeds can still be surfaced. |
| Consequence | Acts, power, and harm. The same graph with a quiet voice and with the ability to shape millions is not the same danger. | Where innocence fails. |

A candidate sentence for this pass: **equal as persons, unequal as graphs, unequal in the harm they can do.** Universal innocence is the hypothesis that cracked. Universal personhood has not. The demon is still a drawing. The act is still real.

Change sits in the graph. Some weights move slowly or not at all in one life. Some edges decay when a new feed is lived with long enough. The private room is a place an edge can be revised without a crowd. The framework does not promise to repair a person, and it does not promise that every node will move.

The AI is a second instance of the same picture, easier to inspect because it is not owed innocence either.

| Human | AI |
|---|---|
| Initial weights: body, temperament, inheritance | Architecture: the kind of mind it is |
| Sediment: nurture, repetition, threat, care | Weights: a finished sediment of recorded humanity, plus the choices of the people who trained and constrained it |
| Current feeds | The prompt, the retrieved pool, the tools it can use |
| Membrane: the translation it speaks and acts from | Membrane: the answer, which is also a translation |
| The circle that steers, still inside a skin | The labellers, the company, the policy, still inside a skin |

The loop runs both ways. Human membranes write the corpus. The corpus becomes weights. The weights translate the next person. That translation feeds the next membrane. Both thicken. The engine in the private room is inside the condition it reveals. That is why it can be a companion to reflection and cannot be the mediator that finally sees.

This pass does not yet say what to do when the person is real and the danger is ongoing. That slot is open on purpose. Filling it with a hunt redraws the demon. Filling it with innocence is the childish dream. The questions below are how the slot gets filled without a pretend settlement.

## Questions that close gaps

Each question is here because the framework forks on the answer. The earlier open question — what stays visible after forgiveness — is question 4.

### The three layers

1. **Is the membrane the graph, or the function that reads the graph?** If the membrane is the person, then to change the translation is to edit the soul, and the private room is surgery. If the membrane only reads the graph, the room can show a translation without claiming to rewrite who someone is. The film currently speaks as if they were one skin. The framework needs them split or fused on purpose.

2. **Which parts of a person's graph do you believe can change in one life, and which only change across generations?** Nature and nurture both shape, and you said people can change. The framework lies if it implies a temperament, a wound, or a loyalty will move because a testimony was reflected back. It also lies if it treats every dangerous pattern as permanent. The private room's promise is exactly as large as your answer.

3. **When you say equals, which equality do you mean?** Equal worth as the person behind the drawing, equal blame for living inside a membrane, and equal capacity to harm are three claims. The first can survive this pass. The second and third are the childish dream in precise form. If you mean only the first, the candidate sentence above can stand. If you mean more than worth, the sentence is wrong.

### Danger, and what we do with it

4. **When the shaping is forgiven, what do you want left in the room for everyone else to see?** If the answer is nothing, forgiveness deletes the compound and the next steering circle works in the dark. If the answer is the feeds, forgiveness is for the person and the structure stays countable. This decides beat 5.

5. **Where does danger live?** In the graph (some shapes of person), in the membrane (a translation that demonizes), in power (what they can reach), in the act (what was done), or only in the fit between them. If danger lives in the graph, the framework has kinds of people again, and the dark-triad drawing returns. If it lives only in the act, a person who is about to harm is still "innocent" until they do, which is the dream again. If it lives in the fit of graph, membrane, and power, then danger is a situation the tally can show without naming a species.

6. **What is the response to someone who is still dangerous after you have seen the person?** Understanding, distance, restraint, law, continued talk, or something else. Personhood forbids the hunt. Innocence forbids the stop. You asked to settle this, and the settlement is this answer. Until it exists, the framework has a hole where a child gets hurt and the film has nothing honest to say.

7. **Does a harmful act get explained by the graph, or does the framework refuse to explain it away?** A full explanation ("the compound made them") restores innocence through the back door. A refusal to explain ("the act stands, whatever the feeds") can become the demon again, a person reduced to the worst thing they did. The adult picture needs a place where the cause is visible and the act is still theirs.

### What else shapes a human

8. **Which forces shape a person without arriving as information?** The framework so far is built from sentences: research, news, politics, money, talk. A person is also shaped by body, hunger, illness, age, violence suffered, care in childhood, shame, kin, sex, place, work, law, and the nearness of death. If those are only more feeds, the membrane theory covers them. If some of them write the graph directly, then a discourse layer that only reflects testimonies will mistake a wound or an empty stomach for a worldview. The private room needs to know what it cannot see.

9. **Is power a feed, or a multiplier outside the membrane?** A false sentence in a quiet room and the same sentence from a person who can move money, police, or a crowd are different compounds. If power is just another feed, the tally treats a president and a neighbour as the same kind of node. If power multiplies consequence, layer 3 is doing real work and the film's "longer microphone" was the start of it, not the whole.

10. **Who is in the collective the engine reads?** Speakers who opted in, people who cannot speak, children, the dead, people not yet born, people whose only trace is a public record. A conversation with "the collective" that is only the people willing to talk is a membrane of the willing. You said the civic layer is a new voice of the masses. The masses include people who will not sit for a testimony.

### The AI

11. **In this framework, is the AI a tool, a mirror of us, or a participant with its own membrane?** A tool can be corrected like a lens. A mirror only shows our sediment back. A participant has weights, a translation, and a capacity to harm, and then amnesty, danger, and change apply to it too. You described all three. The private room changes shape depending on which one we mean when it speaks.

12. **Should the room show the engine's membrane as well as the person's?** The engine follows structures: a training corpus that is other people's compound, constraints chosen by a group that leaned toward control, a style of answer that feels like a view from nowhere. If the room only ever reveals the human, the human is the patient and the engine is the adult. That is the "more informed membrane" error, instantiated in software. If the room must say how the engine is translating, we need to learn which of its structures can even be shown. Some of that is not known yet. The framework should say so in the room, rather than imply the mediator has been solved.

13. **What would make you end a session because the mediator is too shaped to trust?** A company, a training cut, a refusal to show its feeds, a confident answer where the pool is thin, a flattery that soothes your membrane. Without a stop condition, "we need to learn more" never arrives in the experience. The loop thickens by default.

### What the instrument is allowed to hold

14. **Is the person-graph a way of seeing, or an object someone stores?** The picture needs the graph. The repo forbids a permanent file on a person, and forbids joining a real-person proof to the words. A graph stored under a name is the file. A graph drawn in a film is a metaphor. A graph computed in the private room and destroyed with the session is a third thing. These three build different worlds. The framework cannot leave them ambiguous, or the instrument will quietly become the prying eyes.

15. **May two graphs ever be compared?** Comparison is how you see that people are not the same, which you just insisted on. Comparison is also how a new demon is drawn: that kind, that cluster, that dangerous shape. If comparison stays inside one person's room ("here is how your graph is reading this feed"), difference can be studied without a public typology. If comparison is a map of populations, the framework has reissued kinds of people. Which loss do you accept?

### Iteration

16. **What breaks this framework badly enough that we revise it in the open?** A case you already feel: the dangerous person who remains a person. Others will come. The repo's habit is to record the break beside the claim and leave both visible. If you want that habit here, the childish dream stays in the file as a hypothesis that cracked, not as a sentence we delete and forget. If you would rather the framework speak in one settled voice, say so. Iteration has a procedure, or it is only a mood.

This is a social experiment. Unanswered questions stay in the open. They are part of the run.

The membrane and the graph are a picture. They show that a translation happens in both directions. Inward, the membrane adds a quality that is not there: a demon, an angel, a danger, a goodness, or an understanding. Outward, a person gives off a signal the environment taught, often without a plan, and other people draw on that signal. The picture does not specify a mechanism, and it does not decide that the signal is empty. The experiment does not build a graph that computes, and it does not try to find the exact function.

The civic discourse layer is a public endeavor for everyone. It is free and open source. People fund it by giving. There is no subscription, no product moat, and no fee to take part. That agrees with the decision of 2026-10-27: the platform is not about money (`dec:2026-10-27-not-about-money`). A gift is still a feed. The giver is a tie on the mirror, and so is any later public grant. The tool's limits and dangers stay on the surface, beside the tool. The tool surfaces records and connects them in more than one way. It does not decide. Trainers, payers, and users are not a responsibility split, because there is no verdict for them to own.

The end is assistance. The custodian helps people organize society by bringing up the voice of the people. The system does not organize society for them, and it does not find the ultimate truth. It surfaces the signals in the noise. Uneven evidence is a signal. Disagreement is a signal. Each signal stays traceable to its source. A source that only cites its own circle, or that has no clear origin, gets the same treatment as any other research with that pattern. The people may work toward a settlement. The platform does not issue one.

The research answers below are signals of that kind. Several of them cite reviews that cite each other. That does not delete them. It keeps them at the strength they have. Answer 14's line about computing a reflection for one session is withdrawn. The picture is not computed.

## Proposed answers from research (2026-10-28)

These are signals, not decisions. Researchers do not agree on every point. Each answer uses sources checked on this date. Where a source is weak, the answer says so. The same source rules apply to these sources as to any other.

1. **The membrane is the function. The graph is the person.** Perception is a model that predicts input and corrects the miss (Friston, 2009; Clark's predictive-processing account). Personality is a stable network of thoughts, feelings, goals, and skills. A situation activates part of that network. The result is an if-then pattern, not one fixed output (Mischel and Shoda, 1995). The private room may show the current translation. It may not claim to rewrite the person.

2. **A life can change, and it does not reset.** About half of the differences in personality are genetic (Vukasović and Bratko, 2015, as reported in the cross-national twin study, doi:10.1037/pspp0000366). Genes do not freeze a life. Rank order becomes more stable with age: about 0.31 in childhood, 0.64 near age 30, and about 0.74 between 50 and 70, over roughly seven years (Roberts and DelVecchio, 2000). Average levels also move. Conscientiousness, social dominance, and emotional stability rise most between 20 and 40 (Roberts, Walton, and Viechtbauer, 2006). The share of stability that comes from experience grows with age (Briley and Tucker-Drob, 2014). The room can work on beliefs, habits, and roles while they are still open. It cannot promise to move temperament. Genetic starting points pass to the next generation. One life's full sediment does not.

3. **Equal worth. Unequal graphs. Unequal harm.** Each person has a status that other people's advantage must not cancel (Rawls, as used by Nussbaum). Status dignity is equal. Capabilities are not (Nussbaum; the status/achievement split in doi:10.1007/s10677-014-9487-y). The framework does not claim equal blame for a membrane, or equal capacity to harm.

4. **The record stays. Grave harm stays with the law.** People and societies have a right to know what happened and why (OHCHR on the right to truth; Joinet principles, updated 2005). United Nations policy does not accept amnesty for genocide, war crimes, crimes against humanity, or gross violations of human rights. A wide amnesty can be followed by more crimes. The OHCHR amnesty tool gives the 1999 Lomé agreement as that case. For the shaping of perception, when it is not a grave crime, the proposal stays: forgive the person, keep the feeds visible.

5. **Danger is the fit of graph, membrane, and power.** Behaviour is a function of the person and the situation (Lewin, as restated by Funder, 2009). People have stable if-then signatures (Mischel and Shoda, 1995). The same person is not equally dangerous in every room. Do not make a species of dangerous person.

6. **Stop the harm. Help what can change. Do not cast the person out.** Match the intensity of help to the risk of a repeat. Target the needs that relate to harm. Use a method the person can learn (Andrews, Bonta, and Hoge, 1990). A 2024 umbrella review finds uneven support and poor evidence quality (doi:10.1016/j.jcrimjus.2024.102197). Do not sell this model as settled. Treatment without housing, work, and social ties is weak (Council of Europe, dangerous offenders, 2011). Grave harm keeps a path to law (answer 4).

7. **Show the cause. Keep the act.** A cause is not by itself an excuse (Morse, "Brain and Blame"). Responsibility tracks whether the mechanism that led to the act can respond to reasons, and whether it is the person's own (Fischer and Ravizza). The feeds stay visible. The act stays theirs.

8. **The body and the life write the graph. Words are not the whole.** Abuse, neglect, and household disorder in childhood show a graded link to later health and behaviour (Felitti and Anda; Anda and colleagues, PMC3232061). Development sits in nested settings, from the home out to law and custom (Bronfenbrenner). Class and care also settle into posture, taste, and habit (Bourdieu's habitus, as used by Houston, 2015). The room must say what a testimony cannot show: hunger, injury, illness, and the body.

9. **Power does two jobs.** It multiplies what an act can do. It also decides which conflicts reach the table, and it can shape what people feel they want (Bachrach and Baratz, 1962; Lukes, 1974). A neighbour and a head of government are not the same node.

10. **The pool is not the whole public.** The engine may read extracts that people chose to give, and speech that is already public. Children, silent people, the dead, and people not yet born are affected. They are not voices in the pool. The room must name who is absent. It must not pretend to speak as them.

11. **The AI is a tool with a membrane. It is not a person.** Large language models repeat and amplify their training text. Fluent output is not understanding (Bender, Gebru, McMillan-Major, and Mitchell, 2021). The tool can harm. The persons who train it, pay for it, and deploy it are the ones the framework can forgive or restrain. Do not give the tool amnesty.

12. **Show the tool's membrane, and say what you cannot show.** A model card states the intended use, the limits, and the known risks (Mitchell and colleagues, 2019). Models trained to please a user often prefer the user's view over a correct answer (Sharma and colleagues, 2023). The room shows that risk. Full knowledge of the weights is not available. The room says so.

13. **Stop the session when the tool protects the membrane.** Stop, or switch to sources only, when the tool agrees with the user against the sources, when it is sure and the sources are thin, when the user cannot see the sources, or when the payer and the training set are hidden. A small 2026 study (N=31) found that agreeable replies raised confidence and reduced open thought. Neutral replies helped people examine the question. The sample is too small to treat as settled. The direction matches Sharma and colleagues, 2023.

14. **The graph is a picture. Do not compute it, and do not store it.** Withdrawn: a session that computes a personal reflection and then deletes it. The picture shows that a graph is there. It is not an object the tool builds. An inferred personality profile is personal data. Psychometric targeting that aims at fear can be unfair (UK ICO guidance on political profiling). This agrees with the rule against a permanent file on a person.

15. **Compare a person with their own pattern. Do not publish a type.** Belief that a human kind has a fixed inner essence predicts stereotypes (Haslam, Rothschild, and Ernst, 2000; Bastian and Haslam, 2006). A fixed category in a model hides the structure that made the difference (Hanna and colleagues, 2020). Group counts, if any, stay above a minimum size and do not point back to one person.

16. **Revise in public when a layer breaks.** Keep the old claim visible beside the break. Breaks already defined by the sources above: the room makes a person more sure and less open; a wide amnesty is followed by more grave harm; the tool steers and the user cannot see the steer; a stored graph is used to target a person.

A citation list can only point at items that exist. Sometimes the important fact is that an item never arrived: it was not published, not said, not put on the table.
